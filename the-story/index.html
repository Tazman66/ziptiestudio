About Zip Tie Studio

The
Story.

Zip Tie Studio is a classical music production company built by musicians. We exist because the people who make recordings for a living should also understand the music from the inside.

Why We
Exist.

Most recording sessions involve two separate teams — one for audio, one for video. Two setups, two briefs, two invoices, and a post-production problem nobody anticipated. The audio team doesn't know why the cameras are positioned where they are. The video team doesn't know why the microphones are where they are.

We handle both. Microphone placement and camera angle are decided in the same conversation. The result sounds and looks like it belongs together, because it was made that way.

Zip Tie Studio was founded in 2023 by musicians trained at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland. We understand the repertoire, the way sessions run, and what a final result needs to sound and look like.

Based in Glasgow and Edinburgh. Available throughout the UK and internationally.

The Team

Campbell Parker

Director · Audio & Video Producer

Trained as a classical musician at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland. Campbell leads all Zip Tie Studio productions, combining a background in orchestral performance with professional filmmaking and audio engineering. He is also Assistant Orchestra Manager at the Scottish Chamber Orchestra.

Matthew Swan

Audio Engineer & Producer

Matthew brings broadcast and studio experience across classical, contemporary and live settings. His work with Zip Tie Studio spans album sessions, live concert recording and post-production — with an ear trained by years of professional performance as well as behind the console.
