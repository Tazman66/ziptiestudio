About Zip Tie Studio

The
Story.

Zip Tie Studio is a classical music production company built by musicians. We started it because the people who make recordings for a living should also understand the music from the inside.

Most recording sessions involve two separate teams. One for audio, one for video. Two setups, two briefs, two invoices, and a post-production problem nobody anticipated. We handle both. Microphone placement and camera angle are decided in the same conversation. The result sounds and looks like it belongs together, because it was made that way.

We are based in Glasgow and Edinburgh and work throughout Scotland, the UK and internationally. Our clients include internationally recognised ensembles, labels and institutions, and every project, whatever the scale, gets the same level of attention.

The Team

Campbell Parker

Director · Audio & Video Producer

Campbell is a multi-disciplinary creative specialising in filmmaking, photography and audio recording. Trained as a classical musician at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland, he has spent over a decade immersed in the arts as a performer, organiser and producer.

Based in Glasgow and Edinburgh, Campbell works throughout Scotland and beyond with clients including Pentatone, the Dunedin Consort, Scottish Ensemble, the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland and Scottish Young Musicians. Thoughtful, technically assured and deeply embedded in the world of classical music, he brings a musician's sensitivity and a producer's precision to every project.

Matthew Swan

Audio Engineer & Producer

Matthew is a record producer, sound engineer and audio editor with wide experience across the recorded classical music industry. His work spans the full range of the repertoire, from early music on period instruments to premiere recordings of contemporary works, and has featured in Gramophone, BBC Music Magazine and on BBC Radio 3.

His experience in realising the ideas of musicians and composers has led to chart-topping recordings and nominations from the leading international music press. Described by independent producer Andrew Keener as a natural in the recording studio, an empathetic presence whose people skills are as astute as those of his musicianship, Matthew brings rigour, warmth and genuine musical understanding to every session.

We're very pleased with the results and wouldn't hesitate to work with Zip Tie Studio again in future."
