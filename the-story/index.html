About Zip Tie Studio

The
Story.

Zip Tie Studio is a classical music production company built by musicians. We handle audio and video as a single integrated service — because microphone placement and camera angle should be decided in the same conversation.

The Team

Campbell Parker

Director · Audio & Video Producer

Trained at the Royal Conservatoire of Scotland. Campbell leads all productions, combining orchestral performance with professional filmmaking and audio engineering.

Matthew Swan

Audio Engineer & Producer

Audio engineer and producer with broadcast and studio experience across classical and contemporary settings. Matthew's ear is trained by years of professional performance as well as behind the console.
