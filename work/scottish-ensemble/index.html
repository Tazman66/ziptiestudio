THE PROJECT

Our work with Scottish Ensemble spans multiple projects across 2025 and 2026, reflecting an ongoing relationship built on trust and a shared commitment to quality.

For their Candlelight Christmas concert, we provided live vision mixing using the ATEM production suite, delivering a broadcast-ready edited concert film the following morning, available for social and web within 24 hours of the final note.

We also recorded and filmed a side-by-side concert featuring Scottish Ensemble's young artists programme, capturing the energy and precision of the next generation of Scottish chamber musicians.

A third project saw us produce a short documentary film for Scottish Ensemble's partnership with Maggies, the cancer support charity, a film that needed to be warm, human and true to the work both organisations do.
