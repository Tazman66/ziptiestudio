Services

THE PROJECT

Pentatone commissioned us to deliver all visual media for Sean Shibe's 2025 album release. The brief was to create a body of work that could sustain an international release campaign across streaming platforms, social media, press and broadcast.

We produced two music videos featuring Sean performing works from the album, a behind-the-scenes documentary capturing the recording process, and a long-form interview film in which Sean discusses the album, the repertoire and the ideas behind it. The footage was then cut into a series of short-form social edits for Pentatone's channels.

The films were featured by Gramophone as their Video of the Day, and photography from the project appeared in the magazine's May 2026 issue, where Sean was the cover artist.
