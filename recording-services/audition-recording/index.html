The Process

HOW IT WORKS.

01

BOOK A SESSION

Tell us the repertoire, the submission deadline and any technical requirements from the panel. We will find a date that works and confirm the setup in advance.

02

THE SESSION

We set up, do a quick sound check and then get out of the way. Sessions are relaxed. We run as many takes as you need and keep an ear out for anything worth flagging.

03

SELECTION & EDIT

After the session we share the raw takes with you. For edited submissions, you mark the best moments and we assemble them into a clean, seamless result.

04

DELIVERY

Final files delivered within 48 hours for standard submissions. Rush turnaround available for tight deadlines. We deliver in whatever format the panel requires.
