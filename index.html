Classical Music Production · UK & International

We understand the music from the inside. That changes how sessions run, how decisions get made, and what the final result sounds and looks like.

Most sessions involve two teams, one for audio, one for video. Two setups, two briefs, two invoices, and a post-production problem nobody anticipated. We handle both. Microphone placement and camera angle are decided in the same conversation. The result sounds and looks like it belongs together, because it was made that way.

Based in Scotland. Available everywhere.
